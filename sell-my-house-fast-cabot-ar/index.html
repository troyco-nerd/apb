Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I sell my house in Cabot?

Once we've talked through your situation and you've accepted an offer, we can often close in as little as two weeks. The timeline is flexible though – if you need more time, we work around your schedule, not ours.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. We buy homes in any condition – roof issues, foundation concerns, outdated systems, deferred maintenance. None of that needs to be addressed before we make an offer. Sell it as-is and leave the rest to us.

Is there any obligation when I reach out?

None whatsoever. Reaching out to us is simply a conversation. We'll share what we know, walk through your options, and if an offer makes sense, we'll present one. You're under no obligation to accept – or even to respond further.

What if my house needs significant work?

That's actually one of the most common situations we deal with. Properties that need significant work are difficult to sell through traditional channels and expensive to fix up. We account for the property's current condition in our offer and take on all of that ourselves.

What if I'm not ready to sell yet?

That's completely fine. Many people contact us months before they're ready to make a decision. We're happy to have an early conversation, give you a sense of what to expect, and stay in touch until the timing is right for you.

Can I contact you if I'm just exploring options?

Absolutely. Most people who contact us are still in the information-gathering stage – and that's the best time to reach out. The earlier you understand your options, the more flexibility you have. We're happy to be a resource with zero strings attached.
