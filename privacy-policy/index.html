Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Arkansas Property Buyers LLC ("Arkansas Property Buyers," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through arkansaspropertybuyers.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on our Site, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address

  • Property information, such as the address, city, and zip code of a property you're asking about

  • Any additional details you choose to share with us, such as a message describing your situation

  • Your consent choices regarding text messages, described below

If you use our website chat widget, the third-party provider of that widget (GoHighLevel/LeadConnector) may also collect the information you enter into it. Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may process your IP address as part of delivering those fonts. We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and evaluate your property

  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your inquiry, requested information, and appointment scheduling

  • If you've opted in, send occasional promotional or educational messages about property-selling options and homeowner resources

  • Maintain records of our communications with you

How We Share Your Information

We use GoHighLevel as our customer relationship management (CRM) and communications platform. When you submit a form, your information is sent to GoHighLevel so our team can follow up with you. We do not sell your information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding service providers that support the delivery of our messaging services.

Text Message (SMS) Communications

If you check either or both of the consent checkboxes on a form, you agree that Arkansas Property Buyers LLC may send you text messages related to your property inquiry, requested information, appointment scheduling, and/or occasional promotional and educational content about property-selling options and homeowner resources. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive text messages is never required to use our services or to receive a cash offer on your property.

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP, or call us directly at (501) 504-7109.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you've shared with us by calling (501) 504-7109 or by submitting a request through our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
