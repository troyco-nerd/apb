A Starting Point

Understanding Your Options

Every situation is different, and we're not here to give legal or financial advice – that's what attorneys and HUD-approved housing counselors are for. But we can help you understand the general landscape of options that homeowners in your situation often explore.

Loan Modification

Your lender may be willing to modify the terms of your loan – adjusting your interest rate, extending your term, or rolling missed payments into your balance. Many lenders prefer this over the cost and complexity of foreclosure.

Repayment Plan

Some lenders will allow you to catch up on missed payments gradually through a structured repayment plan, giving you time to stabilize your finances without losing your home.

Refinancing

If you have equity in your home, refinancing into a new loan with more favorable terms may reduce your monthly payment and help you get back on track.

Traditional Sale

If time allows, listing your home with a real estate agent may allow you to pay off the mortgage in full and walk away with any remaining equity – protecting your credit in the process.

Sell Directly to Arkansas Property Buyers

If a faster, simpler path makes more sense for your situation, we may be able to purchase your property directly – as-is, on a timeline that works for you, with no repairs, no showings, and no agent commissions. We can move quickly when the situation calls for it.

Note: Arkansas Property Buyers does not provide legal or financial advice. We encourage homeowners to consult with a qualified attorney or HUD-approved housing counselor for guidance specific to their situation.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell my house if I'm behind on payments?

In many cases, yes. Being behind on payments does not automatically prevent a sale. The proceeds from the sale may be used to pay off the outstanding mortgage balance, and in some situations this can be a practical way to avoid foreclosure and protect your credit. Every situation is different, and we'd encourage you to reach out so we can discuss what may apply to yours.

Is it too late if I've already received notices from my lender?

Not necessarily. Receiving a notice of default or even a notice of sale does not always mean options are exhausted. The foreclosure process in Arkansas takes time, and depending on where you are in that process, there may still be alternatives available. The sooner you reach out, the more options are likely to be on the table.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. If you sell directly to us, we purchase properties as-is. There's no need to make repairs, clean out the property, or do any updates. We handle everything after closing so you don't have to.

What if my home needs significant work?

That's not a problem for us. We buy homes in all conditions – including properties that need major repairs, have deferred maintenance, or haven't been updated in years. The condition of the property does not need to be addressed before we can have a conversation.

How quickly can the process move?

We can move quickly when a situation calls for it. In some cases we've been able to close in a matter of days. That said, we work on your timeline – if you need more time, we're flexible. The first step is simply having a conversation so we understand your situation and what timeline makes sense.

Is there any obligation if I contact you?

None at all. Reaching out costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. We're simply here to have an honest conversation about your situation and help you understand what options may be available. You decide what happens next, on your own timeline.
