Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does selling my house as-is actually mean?

It means you sell your property in its current condition without making repairs, renovations, or major updates. We purchase the home exactly as it sits, so you don't have to spend thousands of dollars fixing things before selling.

Do I need to make any repairs?

No. We purchase properties as-is. Whether your home has minor cosmetic issues or major structural needs, we buy it in its current condition. No repairs, no cleanout, no contractor coordination needed.

What if the property needs major work?

That's not a problem for us. We buy homes in all conditions – including properties that need major repairs, have significant deferred maintenance, or haven't been updated in years. Major repairs don't disqualify your home from an offer.

Do I need to clean everything out?

No. You can take the belongings you want to keep. We'll work with you to handle whatever remains in the property – whether that's items you'd like us to remove, donate, or dispose of. We discuss what makes sense for your situation.

How quickly can we close?

We can close quickly when that's what you need – in some cases, within weeks. But we also work with longer timelines if you need more time to prepare. You choose the closing date that works best for your situation.

Is there any obligation if I contact you?

None at all. Reaching out costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. We're here to answer questions, discuss your situation, and help you understand what options might be available. You decide what happens next, on your own timeline.

Still have questions? We're here to help.

No pressure. No commitment. Just honest answers from a local Arkansas team that understands your situation.
